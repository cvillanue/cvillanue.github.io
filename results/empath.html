Human Behavior Scenarios

Three simulated conditions exercise the human-behavior and assistance decision pipeline. These are software simulation runs, not results from recruited human participants.

ScenarioObserved behaviorOutcome
DeliberationCorrect block selected without prior errorsTask complete
DifficultyTwo incorrect block selectionsAssistance candidate
Self-recoveryEarly errors followed by a correct selectionTask complete

Decision Policy Example

In the difficulty scenario, the system initially chose to wait after one incorrect selection and later chose to offer assistance after the second. This demonstrates the prototype decision pipeline; it does not establish performance with real users.

Next Steps

Extend validation to more varied behaviors and report model calibration, intervention performance, and robustness across scenarios.
